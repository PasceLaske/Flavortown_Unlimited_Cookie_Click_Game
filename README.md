# Hack Club Flavortown Cookie Clicker

🍪 **The ultimate idle clicking game with maximum Flavortown energy!**  
Build insane projects, click cookies like Guy Fieri, unlock AI-powered flavor bots and collect ridiculous achievements — all in the spirit of Hack Club & Flavortown!



## Features

- Classic cookie clicking with **huge scaling**
- Flavortown-themed upgrades (Dino's BBQ Bot, Triple D Factory, Quantum Flavor Blaster, ...)
- **AI-generated flavor bots** — unlock increasingly powerful bots forever
- Exponential pricing & power scaling (never ends!)
- **Large number abbreviation system** (1a, 1b, 1z, aa, ab, ..., aaa, ...)
- Golden Cookies that appear randomly for big bonuses
- **Milestone achievements** (unlocked at 5 / 10 / 20 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1000+ bots and new number tiers like aa, bb, ...)
- Beautiful animated background & glowing title
- Mobile friendly (works on phones & tablets)

## How to Play

1. Click the giant 🍪 to earn cookies
2. Buy upgrades to increase cookies per second (cps)
3. Unlock new **AI Flavor Bots** when you have enough cookies
4. Watch your cps skyrocket into the aa, bb, aaa ranges
5. Catch **Golden Cookies** for surprise bonuses
6. Collect epic achievements and become the ultimate Flavor Lord

## Controls

- **Left click** on the cookie → +1 cookie (or more with upgrades)
- Click upgrades/bots to purchase them
- Wait and watch numbers go brrrrr

## Technologies

- Pure **HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JavaScript**
- No frameworks, no build tools — just open `index.html` in your browser
- Responsive design
- Math for very large numbers (exponential growth & abbreviation)
